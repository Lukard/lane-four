Lane 4

Hurdles in your living room. Jog in place to run, squat to jump. Your camera does the tracking.

  • Set upPut your phone or laptop at about waist height, 2 to 3 m away, so you're in the picture from head to feet.
  • RunJog or march in place. Every step moves your runner, and faster steps mean more speed.
  • JumpSquat as a hurdle comes up and stand to jump. Hold the squat and you'll jump at the hurdle. A deeper squat jumps higher and can grab the star.

Video is processed on this device and never uploaded. The tracking model downloads from Google's servers the first time.
